# Japanese outward FDI: ASEAN destinations and industries, 2024

Source: Ministry of Finance, Outward / Inward Direct Investment, breakdown by
Region and Industry (Annual totals), 2024 calendar year.

- Published table: https://www.mof.go.jp/policy/international_policy/reference/balance_of_payments/revised/bprfdi20240112.pdf
- Excel: https://www.mof.go.jp/policy/international_policy/reference/balance_of_payments/revised/fdi2024.xlsx
- Index: https://www.mof.go.jp/english/policy/international_policy/reference/balance_of_payments/ebpfdii.htm

The CSV transcribes the published numeric table inspected on 2026-11-18.
Page numbers are one-based: page 1 contains regional/industry totals and page 3
contains the six ASEAN destination economies shown on the slide.

The comparison was extended to selected major destinations outside ASEAN:
China (page 3), India (page 4), United States (page 5), Australia (page 7), and
United Kingdom, Netherlands and Germany (page 8). It is a selected comparison,
not an exhaustive ranking of the world's largest recipients. Singapore is
included in both the worldwide comparison and the detailed ASEAN chart.

Values are annual net outward FDI flows, in 100 million yen. Divide by 10 for
billions of yen. They are compiled on the directional principle, not the
asset/liability principle of the headline balance-of-payments series. Do not
mix their denominators with the existing MOF financial-account charts.

Non-manufacturing includes service industries but also agriculture, mining,
construction and other industries; it is not synonymous with services.
The four selected service industries are components of non-manufacturing and
must not be added to its total. The source's narrow category "Services" is
not a total for all service industries and is deliberately not used as such.

Source totals may differ slightly from the sum of rounded components. Use the
published Total rows for regional shares and displayed totals. The chart shows
six selected ASEAN economies; the ASEAN aggregate also includes other members
covered by the source. The source's country classification for 2024 is retained.
