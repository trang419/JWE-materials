Course Syllabus: Japan in the World Economy

Author

Trang Tran

Week 1: Introduction

  • Course objectives and expectations
  • Japan’s position in the global economy

Week 2: Japan’s Comparative Advantages

  • Structure of exports and imports (manufacturing, agriculture, services)
  • Trade links with major partners (U.S., EU, China, ASEAN)

Week 3: Measurement of Global Value Chains

  • What is global value chain?
  • How to measure it?
  • Japan’s role in GVCs (automobiles, electronics, advanced manufacturing)

Week 4: Enablers of Global Value Chains

  • Trade policy frameworks (RCEP, CPTPP, WTO)
  • Domestic policies for competitiveness and innovation
  • Infrastructure needs for integration into supply chains

Week 5: Japan and ASEAN

  • Japan–ASEAN trade and investment ties
  • ASEAN’s role in regional production networks
  • Cooperation frameworks for supply chain resilience

Week 6: Trade Tensions and Climate Change

  • U.S.–China rivalry and Japan’s position
  • De-globalization and supply chain diversification
  • Climate change as a global economic challenge

Week 7: mid-term

Week 8: Official Development Assistance

  • ODA for regional diplomacy and infrastructure finance
  • How ODA reinforce Japan’s role

Week 9: Cross-border investment

  • Foreign Direct Investment (FDI)
  • Outward FDI in ASEAN manufacturing and services
  • Inward FDI and domestic challenges

Week 10: Balance of Payments

  • Balance of payments
  • Capital flows and capital controls

Week 11: Exchange Rates

  • Yen exchange rate and competitiveness in GVCs
  • Use of yen in trade settlement and finance

Week 12: Demographic Challenges

  • Labor shortages and productivity challenges
  • Role of technology in trade facilitation
  • Opportunities and barriers for Japan’s digital transformation

Week 13: Final exam

Assessment and Assignments

  • Short Tests: 30%
  • Mid-term: 30%
  • Final exam: 40%

Readings and Resources

  • Ito, T and Hoshi, T (2020). The Japanese Economy

  • Krugman, P. R., Obstfeld, M., & Melitz, M. J. (2023). International economics: Theory and policy (12th ed.). Pearson.

  • Papers/Reports introduced in lessons
